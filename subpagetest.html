Return

A-200 is an entity that takes on the wind archetype. Originally from Rooms (a game by Nicorocks5555 who was terminated and privated), A-200 has gotten a redesign and is more cleaner that its old design. A-200 will spawn a few rooms ahead and, after a few seconds, start chasing anyone alive not hiding. If there are no valid targets to chase, it will continue going in the direction it was moving.If close enough to an alive player, A-200 will instantly kill them if they are not hiding.As of the corporate restructure update, A-200 also summons it's fog. this means that alive players will start losing oxygen when hiding (indicated by a red vignette on the screen). If the player runs out of oxygen (the screen turning completely red while suffocating), they will die. Oxygen regenerates when not hiding, or when the fog isn't active.Surviving A-200 requires managing your oxygen by timing when you hide from A-200, from hiding for long periods of time to get A-200 a greater distance from you, or hiding really quickly to not spend too much oxygen, the best advice is to not hide immediately when it spans, but as soon as it gets close. It also has a sound that can be heard when it is very close, which can help.A-200's whole thing is basically copied from Rooms & Doors, from the mechanic, to even it's close sound.There was planned to be a modifier which would make the fog always present, but modifiers were never added.A-200 was supposed to use the frames from Rooms.A-200 is an entity that takes on the wind archetype.
